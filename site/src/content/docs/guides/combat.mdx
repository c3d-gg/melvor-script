Your script can fight anything you could fight by hand: pick a style, gear up, fight, eat, collect the loot. This guide goes through it in that order. Every call here is in the [reference](/reference/combat/), with what costs a cooldown.

## Gear up

There's no separate "cast" or "shoot" call: **your weapon decides how you attack.** Equip a sword and every attack is a melee hit, a bow and it's an arrow, a staff and it's a spell. Then pick an attack style, which decides where the combat XP goes.

Equipping, attack styles and spells are all free: no cooldown.

  <TabItem label="Scripted Mode">
    Combat is turn-based. `fight` attacks for you, one round per cooldown, until it has the kills. For control over **every round**, attack yourself: each `attack()` is one round, where you swing and then the monster swings.

    <Snippet file="site/snippets/guides/combat.ts" region="turn-based" />

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Normal character">
    Combat runs in real time, as when you fight by hand. `fight` waits for the kills, then leaves combat. `start(monster)` begins a fight that keeps going until you call `stop()` (or die).

</Tabs>

## Stay alive

<Aside type="caution" title="fight() doesn't eat for you">
Unless you have the game's **Auto Eat**, nothing eats during `fight`. A script that only calls `fight` will keep swinging until the character dies.
</Aside>

Equip food, then watch your HP while the fight runs. `m.onState` calls your function every time the game sends new state, several times a second, and eating is free, so it can happen in the middle of a fight:

<Snippet file="site/snippets/guides/combat.ts" region="eat-while-fighting" />

The turn-based loop above does the same between rounds. Either way, pick a monster your level can handle: `m.data.monsters()` lists every monster's combat level.

## Loot, prayers and potions

None of these cost a cooldown, so do them whenever you like: before a fight, between kills, or in the middle of one.

<Snippet file="site/snippets/guides/combat.ts" region="loot-prayer-potion" />

Potions last a number of charges. `m.potions.setAutoReuse('melvorD:Combat')` drinks a new one from the bank when the old one runs out.

## Slayer tasks

A Slayer task names a monster and a number of kills. `m.combat.slayer.fight()` fights your task's monster until the task is done. After a task, the game picks the next one in the same tier for free, and `ensureTask` takes it, so a loop never pays for a new task by accident.

<Snippet file="examples/slayer.ts" />

`newTask(tier)` swaps your task for a new one (Easy tasks are free to swap), and `extend()` adds kills to the current one for Slayer Coins.
