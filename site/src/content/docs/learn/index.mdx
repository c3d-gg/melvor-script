This course is for people who have **never written code**. You'll learn TypeScript, a real language used for websites, apps and tools, by making your Melvor character do things. Every lesson starts with something you want in the game and teaches the one programming idea you need to get it.

If you already write code, the [quick start](/start/quick-start/) is faster.

## How the lessons work

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A goal first" icon="star">
    Each lesson opens with what you'll make and what you'll learn. No theory without a reason.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Code you can run" icon="seti:typescript">
    Every example is a complete script. Copy it, run it, watch your character do it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New ideas, one at a time" icon="information">
    New words and ideas get a box of their own, explained in plain language the first time they appear.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Challenges" icon="puzzle">
    Each lesson ends with small tasks to try on your own. A solution is hidden under each, for when you're stuck.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip" title="You can't break anything">
The worst a script can do is stop with an error message, and the message says what went wrong. Try things. Change numbers. See what happens.
</Aside>

## The lessons

1. [**Get set up**](/learn/01-setup/): install everything and run your first script. <Badge text="Ready" variant="success" />
2. [**Your first script**](/learn/02-first-script/): chop ten trees. Calling functions, `await`, text. <Badge text="Ready" variant="success" />
3. [**Loops and variables**](/learn/03-loops/): chop until you have 100 logs. `while`, `for`, `let`. <Badge text="Ready" variant="success" />
4. **Making decisions**: eat when your HP is low, sell when the bank is full. `if` and `else`. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
5. **Functions**: cut logs then burn them, as one reusable step. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
6. **Lists and objects**: find the best tree you can cut. Arrays, `filter`, `sort`. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
7. **When things go wrong**: read errors and handle them. `try` and `catch`. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
8. **Two things at once**: farm in the background while you chop. Events. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
9. **Project: a Slayer bot**: a magic staff, food, tasks back to back. Everything together. <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="note" />
