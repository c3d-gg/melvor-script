## Fair by design

Your script can't go faster than the rules allow. In **Scripted Mode**, every action happens the moment you ask, then a cooldown starts: the action's normal time divided by a speed you choose. A loop that tries to spam simply waits. On a normal character, actions take their usual time. [How it works](/concepts/how-it-works/) explains the rules.

It's a programming game in the spirit of Screeps, Bitburner and Adventure Land, inside an idle game you might already play.
